Scribe of Lagash

Small, focused Obsidian plugins for writers who would rather be writing than configuring.

A series of free plugins for Obsidian. Visualize your story, track your goals, and write without distractions — each plugin does one thing, needs almost no setup, and leaves your notes exactly as you wrote them.

Why Scribe of Lagash See the plugins
// why

Less tool. More writing.

Scribe of Lagash was born from trying the other tools. They were powerful, but every one came with dozens of settings, panels and features a writer rarely needs — and before you could write a single scene, you had to learn the tool.

Scribe of Lagash goes the other way: use only what your writing approach needs, with as little configuration as possible. The tool gets out of the way so you can sit down and start writing.

— made by Alejandro Escanes, software engineer / writer

// in detail

A closer look at each plugin.

01 — visualization

See the whole story at a glance.

Chapters and scenes stay as regular notes in your vault. Point the plugin at your story folder and it recognizes them from their titles — "Chapter 1", "Scene II", "Prologue" — with no extra setup. It never edits a note you already wrote.

  • StoryLines — every chapter and scene as a card on colored lines you create; drag cards between lines and along a shared column grid.
  • Plot matrix — your Story Outline as an editable table; click a planned row to turn it into a real chapter or scene note.
  • Characters & Locations — the same cards grouped by who appears in them and where they happen.
  • Chronological — cards ordered by in-story date, so flashbacks and parallel timelines are easy to follow.
Full documentation on GitHub →
02 — goals and stats

Keep a steady writing habit.

Choose a story folder, a daily goal in words or characters, and the days you write. Weekly and monthly targets are worked out for you. Your history lives in a file inside your vault, so it travels with it.

  • Writing-goal widget — a ring for today's progress, a week and month summary, and a calendar that colors each day by whether you met your goal.
  • Goals and Stats tab — week, month, rolling 7- and 30-day and yearly totals with daily averages.
  • Writing session — a simple countdown timer with 15, 30 and 60-minute presets that survives closing Obsidian.
  • File-explorer counts — word or character counts next to every note and folder, updating as you write.
  • A small celebration — a toast and a burst of confetti the moment you reach the daily goal.
Full documentation on GitHub →
03 — focus

Just you and the page.

Press your hotkey and everything except the current note disappears: sidebars, ribbon, tabs and status bar. On desktop the window goes full screen too. Press Esc to come back. There is no settings tab, and your notes are never edited.

  • Four focus modes — Distraction-free, Typewriter (the current line stays centered), Spotlight (only the current paragraph is lit) and Deep work (only the current line).
  • Themes — Paper, Sepia, Dark and Night pages, without touching your Obsidian theme.
  • The dock — a small bar at the bottom to switch mode, font, text size, theme and key sounds — it hides while you write.
  • Key sounds — optional recordings of a real mechanical keyboard as you type.
  • No red squiggles — spell check is switched off while you write, and back on when you leave.
Full documentation on GitHub →

Start writing in a minute.

Requires Obsidian 1.10.0 or later. Install from Settings → Community plugins, or download main.js, manifest.json and styles.css from each plugin's latest release.

2. pick your story folder
3. write.
